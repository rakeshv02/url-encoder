Frequently Asked Questions

What is URL encoding?
URL encoding converts special characters into a format that can be safely transmitted in a URL, replacing them with % followed by hex codes.
Does my data get saved?
No — all processing is done locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
Is this free?
Yes — completely free, no sign-up needed.
Difference between encodeURI and encodeURIComponent?
encodeURI preserves valid URL characters like / and :, while encodeURIComponent encodes everything except letters, digits and - _ . ~
